How to play

Welcome to the class nobody should have graduated from

  1. Put this on the big screen and add everyone in the room.
  2. A superlative comes up. “Most Likely to Sext Their Boss by Accident.” On 3, 2, 1, VOTE! everyone points at who deserves it. No voting for yourself.
  3. Tap whoever got the most fingers. Tie? Tap them all: each gets 15 seconds to argue why they don’t deserve it, then vote again.
  4. Draw their yearbook photo. The player on the winner’s left gets 60 seconds on the screen (finger, mouse or controller). Unflattering is encouraged.
  5. Yearbook quote. The winner says it, somebody types it in, word for word. Then they take a sip (or the group picks a dare).
  6. Graduation. Most superlatives = Valedictorian of Bad Decisions, speech required. Fewest = “Most Likely to Be Hiding Something.” Then save the whole yearbook as one picture.
