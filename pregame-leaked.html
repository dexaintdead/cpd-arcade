How to play

Somebody leaked the group chats

Sixteen of them. Each one has blanks, and you’re about to fill them with the worst words you can think of, without knowing what they’re for. Then the chat plays out on screen, and you perform it. Out loud. With voices.

Party mode

  1. Add the players. Put this on the big screen.
  2. Pick a leak. The game casts real people from the room as MOM, THE EX, the GYM BRO…
  3. Fill the blanks. The screen asks each player in turn for a word: “Dana, give me a body part.” Nobody sees the chat yet. Stuck? Hit 🎲 for a random word.
  4. Perform the leak. Messages pop in one at a time (tap, Space or Ⓐ). Each cast member reads their own lines. Turn on 🗣 Read aloud if nobody’s brave.
  5. Screenshot of the night. Vote for the funniest word. Whoever gave it gets a ⭐. First to 5 stars wins. Last place reads the next one as a voice memo.

Solo

Chaos mode fills every blank with something unhinged from the word bank. Or fill it yourself. Then 📸 save the screenshot and send it to the real group chat.

Got the printable?

Same 16 chats as the Leaked book from Pregame Papers. Print the pages for the table, run the game on the TV, or mix them: the book’s 2 write-your-own pages are yours to fill.
