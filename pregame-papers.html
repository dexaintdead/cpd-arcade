LEAKED

Game 02 · Class of Bad Decisions

The Unhinged Yearbook

3–12 PLAYERS44 SUPERLATIVESDRAWING21+

Vote your friends into crude yearbook superlatives (“Most Likely to Sext Their Boss by Accident”), draw their yearbook photo in 60 seconds, and make them give a quote. Most wins is Valedictorian of Bad Decisions.
