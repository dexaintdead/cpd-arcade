About the Arcade

The ContentPad Arcade is a free collection of original games you play right in your browser. Everything in it was made in ContentPad's own studio: the art, the characters, the voices and the music, with the code written by Claude.

Play with your keyboard, plug in a controller, or pick up your phone. Sign in with your email and your saves and leaderboard scores follow you to any device.

Many of the games are made with partners: a toy shop in the East Village, a family farm in New Jersey, a Hell's Kitchen nightclub, a party-game brand. Have a brand, a store, a character or a story that should be playable? Make a game with us. See every game.

Is the ContentPad Arcade free?

Yes. Every game is free to play in your web browser at arcade.contentpad.io, with nothing to download. The casino uses play money only.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes. Every game works with a standard USB or Bluetooth game controller, and every game also plays with a keyboard. Most play on a phone with touch controls.

Who makes the games?

ContentPad Studios, the in-house studio of ContentPad. The art, characters, voices and music are made in ContentPad Studio and the code is written by Claude. Many games are made with partners such as Toy Tokyo, Oak Hill Farm, Harbor NYC, Slapwoods and Pregame Papers.

Can my brand get a game in the Arcade?

Yes. ContentPad builds games with brands, shops, venues and creators and puts them in front of everyone in the Arcade. Email support@contentpad.io.
