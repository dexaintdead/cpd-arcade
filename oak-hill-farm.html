    How to play

    Move: arrow keys / WASD, a controller stick, or drag anywhere on the left of the screen.

    Do things: Space or Enter, controller (A), or the big yellow button. Hold it to brush, spray the hose, rake, or break ice.

    Truck: T or F, controller (X), or the truck button. The truck bed holds 4 things!

    Two players: turn it on in Settings. Player 1 uses WASD + Space (F = truck). Player 2 uses the arrow keys + Enter (/ = truck). Two controllers work too.

    Farm Map: 🏠 or H. Album: L or controller (Y). Pause: Esc, P or Start. Sound: M.

    Tap a chore on your clipboard to hear it read out loud. Follow the bouncing arrow if you get lost!

    🖍️ Coloring

    Pick a page to color. Tap a space to fill it with color, or draw with the crayon.
