    How to play

    Move: arrow keys / WASD, a controller stick, or drag anywhere on the left of the screen.

    Do things: Space or Enter, controller (A), or the big yellow button. Hold it to brush an animal or rake up a mess.

    Truck: Y or T, controller (X), or the truck button. The truck bed holds 4 things!

    Album: L, controller (Y). Pause: Esc, P or Start. Sound: M.

    Pick things up at the hay stack, feed shed, water pump, garden and farm stand, then bring them to the animals on your clipboard. Follow the bouncing arrow if you get lost!
