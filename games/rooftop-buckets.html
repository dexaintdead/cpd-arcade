About the game

It's 1986 and the Rooftop Buckets on Sunset just locked its doors. You're the night clerk, and the floor is covered in receipts. Pick up every one, aisle by aisle, while the four mall cops working the graveyard shift hunt you through the store: Rusty comes straight at you, Peaches cuts you off four tiles ahead, Big Sal hangs back until you're close, and Dizzy just wanders, which is somehow worse.

Grab a slushie from the machine and for a few seconds the tables turn: the cops go blue and you can send them back to the break room for 200, 400, 800, 1,600 points. Invented snacks turn up on the floor twice a night for bonus points, from Choco Nuggs to the Key to the Loop. Clear the store and the lights flicker: it's a new night, the cops are faster, and every third night the store changes layout. Three stores: Aisle One, The Freezer and Night Shift. An extra clerk at 10,000 and every 15,000 after.

Bring a friend: press 2 on the title screen for co-op, one clerk on the arrows and one on WASD, or one controller each. Lives are shared, so look after each other. Your high score and deepest night follow you when you sign in to the Arcade.

Questions

Is Rooftop Buckets free?

Yes. Rooftop Buckets is free to play in your web browser on arcade.contentpad.io. There is nothing to download or install.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes. Plug in or pair a standard USB or Bluetooth controller and the d-pad or left stick moves the clerk. A second controller plays the second clerk in co-op.

Does it have two players?

Yes. Press 2 on the title screen for co-op: one clerk on the arrow keys, one on WASD, or one controller each. Lives are shared.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Swipe anywhere on the store, or use the on-screen pad. Add the Arcade to your Home Screen to play full screen.

Is it based on an existing game?

It is an original ContentPad Arcade game in the maze-chase genre, set in the YESTERDAYLAND world with its own three store layouts, its own four mall cops and invented products. No licensed characters.
