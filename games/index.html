Questions

What is the ContentPad Arcade?

The ContentPad Arcade (arcade.contentpad.io) is a free collection of original browser games made in ContentPad's own creative studio, many of them with brand partners. There are 10 games right now and new ones arrive often.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Every game runs in your web browser on a computer, phone or tablet.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes. Every game supports a standard USB or Bluetooth game controller, and every game also plays with a keyboard. Most play on a phone with touch controls.

Are the games free?

Yes, all of them. The casino uses play money only.

Can my brand get a game?

Yes. ContentPad builds games with brands, shops, venues and creators, then puts them in the Arcade for everyone to play. Email support@contentpad.io.

Make a game with us

Every game here was made in ContentPad's own studio, often with a partner: a toy shop, a farm, a nightlife group, a party-game brand. If you have a brand, a character, a store or a story that should be playable, we'd love to build it with you and put it in front of everyone. Email support@contentpad.io.
